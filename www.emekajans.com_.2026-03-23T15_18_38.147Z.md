### OUR SERVICES & SOLUTIONS

## Data Driven Digital Marketing Solutions

Data-driven digital marketing aims to create more effective ads using data to understand customers' behavior, preferences and searches. Such advertising solutions monitor the websites visited by customers, search terms and social media usage, and identify their interests and needs.

​

Data-driven digital marketing is also used to analyze customers' behavior and preferences, to understand their purchasing journeys and to target more accurately. For example, if a customer has searched for a product and is browsing your website, showing him relevant ads will make him more likely to buy it. Such solutions provide higher conversion rates and higher ROIs.

## Corporate Identity & Creative Services

Corporate identity design includes the design of all visual and written materials of a company or organization. These materials include different types of designs such as logos, business cards, brochures, websites and email signatures. Corporate identity design is important for building the image of a company or organization and helps consumers recognize, recognize and trust the company.

​

Brand creative design services include designs used to create the image and identity of a company or product. These designs include different types of designs such as logos, labels, packaging, advertising campaigns and websites. Brand creative design services are important for building the image of a company or product and help consumers recognize, recognize and trust the company or product. With these services, the brand achieves a striking appearance that is compatible with its corporate identity.

### PR & Corporate Communication Services

PR (Public Relations) services aim to communicate with a company or organization's top management, customers, investors and other important public groups. This communication enables the company or organization to announce its aims, activities and services to the public and to create a positive image. PR services include different types of activities such as press releases, press conferences, interviews and special events.

​

Corporate communication services, on the other hand, cover the internal and external communication activities of a company or organization. These activities include different types of communication such as internal company communication, customer communication, investor communication and social media communication. Corporate communication services are essential to accurately and effectively communicate the goals and activities of the company or organization to its internal and external auditors. With these services, the company accurately announces its corporate identity, goals and business activities to the public.

### Media Purchasing & Planning Services

Media buying involves the purchase of advertising spaces as a result of agreements with advertising publishers. These areas include different types of media channels such as television, radio, newspaper, magazine, internet and social media. The media buying process helps advertisers choose the media channels that best suit their target audience and get the most affordable prices.

​

Media planning, on the other hand, involves advertisers determining the strategies and tools needed to deliver their ads in the most effective way. These strategies include different stages such as determining the target audience, choosing media channels, placing advertisements and following activities. Media planning helps advertisers identify the tools and strategies needed to deliver their ads most effectively. This process ensures that the ads reach the target audience in the most effective way and their results are monitored.

### Event & Organization Management

Event management services include the process of planning, organizing and running an event. These services include different stages such as event design, venue selection, speaker and artist invitations, ticket sales, food and beverage arrangements, safety and health measures. Event and organization management services cover all the stages necessary for the successful performance of an event.

​

Organization management services include pre-planning and coordinating all the details of the event. These services include different stages such as project management, cost tracking, time management, human resources management, technical support, corporate communication and marketing. Organization management services ensure that all the necessary details are fulfilled during the realization of the event. Thanks to these services, events are held regularly, professionally and successfully.
