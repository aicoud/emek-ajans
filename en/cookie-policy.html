EMEK AJANS COOKIE POLICY

As Emek Ajans (“Company”), we use cookies, pixels, beacons and similar technologies to improve your user experience and increase efficiency during your visits to our online channels. This Cookie Policy explains the types and purposes of data collected during your use of our website (www.emekajans.com).

1. What is a Cookie?

Cookies are very small text files saved on your device (computer, phone, tablet) through your browser when you visit a website. These files allow our site to recognize you and offer you a special service by remembering your preferences (language, login information, etc.) on your next visit.

2. Purposes of Using Cookies

Cookies used on our site are generally processed for the following purposes:

  • Functionality and Necessity: To fulfill basic functions (secure login, etc.) of the site.
  • Performance and Analysis: To measure performance by anonymously analyzing visitor traffic (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Personalization: To offer a smoother experience by remembering user preferences.
  • Advertising and Marketing: To offer customized advertisements based on interests.

3. Types of Cookies Used on Our Site

Cookie CategoryDescription
Necessary CookiesTechnically necessary for the site to function. Cannot be disabled.
Analytical CookiesAllows us to track which pages are popular.
Targeting CookiesUsed to show you relevant advertisements.
Session CookiesTemporary cookies deleted when you close the browser.

4. Control and Management of Cookies

Your preferences regarding the use of cookies belong entirely to you. You can delete existing cookies from your browser settings or block cookies completely. It should be noted that blocking cookies may prevent some features or services on our site from working at full capacity.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject and Contact

In accordance with Article 11 of the KVKK No. 6698, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed and to request its correction/deletion. You can send all your questions and requests regarding cookies to info@emekajans.com.
