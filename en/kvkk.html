This Policy prepared by our company has been prepared in accordance with the Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”). III. Basic Rules Regarding Processing of Personal Data

  • Compliance with the law and rules of honesty: Our company questions the source of the data it collects or receives and attaches importance to obtaining it within the framework of honesty rules.

  • Processing for specific, explicit and legitimate purposes: Data is processed only for purposes for which approval has been received.
  • Being connected, limited and measured: Data is used only to the extent required by the service.
  • Retention for the period required: Data is kept for legal periods or for the time required for the purpose of processing.

Rights of the Data Subject (KVKK Art. 11)

Real persons whose personal data is processed have the following rights:

  • To know the third parties to whom personal data is transferred domestically or abroad,
  • To request correction of incomplete or incorrectly processed data,
  • To request deletion or destruction of personal data.

Personal data is confidential and our company complies with this confidentiality.
