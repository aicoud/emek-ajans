Our Story

Since 1967.
A legacy built on trust.

Emek Ajans was founded in 1967 in Istanbul. Over more than half a century, we have evolved into a full-service 360° integrated communications agency.

We combine our deep market knowledge with next-generation digital capabilities — SEO, performance marketing, web design, and data analytics — to deliver measurable results.

As an 8-year Google Partner and Meta Business Partner, we help brands grow across digital and traditional channels with equal expertise.
